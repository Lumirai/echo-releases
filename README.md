# Lumirai Echo

**Your team opens the images. Echo puts them in your hands.**

Lumirai Echo is a purpose-built browser for Medstrat Joints that brings cross-device tab sync to
your PACS workflow. When your team opens a study on one device, it appears instantly on every
synced device — images loaded and ready to go.

Echo syncs through the Microsoft 365 for business subscription and OneDrive your practice already
has. There is no server to deploy and no interface engine to configure.

## What Echo does

- **Shared tabs.** Your staff pulls up a study on their device; it appears on yours — same patient,
  same study, already loaded.
- **Notes on a tab.** The side, the complaint, what changed since the last visit, so you arrive
  with the picture and the context at once.
- **Patient search.** Go straight to a patient and their studies.
- **Studies in tabs.** Keep several open at once and move between them.
- **History.** The studies you opened recently, a click away.
- **Your PACS, unchanged.** Echo doesn't replace your imaging system — it keeps working exactly as
  it does, minus the loading.

## Download for macOS

**[Get the latest version](https://github.com/Lumirai/echo-releases/releases/latest)** — open the
`.dmg` and drag Echo into your Applications folder. Builds are signed by Lumirai LLC and notarized
by Apple, and Echo keeps itself up to date from there. The `.zip` beside the `.dmg` is the format
the updater uses, not an installer.

The Windows version comes from the Microsoft Store, which handles installation and updates.

This repository holds Echo's macOS installers and update feed. It does not hold application code.
